How to play

Drag the box anywhere, even while the robot walks to it. Double-click the floor (or T + click) to move the green target. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
R reset · N random · Space pause · . step · P push

Known limits: trained on cubes only, so very flat boxes (under ~15 cm tall) are often not lifted (v16 handles ~85% of 15–30 cm parcels zero-shot). Very large boxes (>45 cm) are occasionally carried to the target but not released.

The robot never gets the box pose: it sees a 32×18 head depth image and an 8×8 cloud of box points, plans hand/foot contacts on what it sees, and tracks them. Stages: search → approach / grip → carry → release.
